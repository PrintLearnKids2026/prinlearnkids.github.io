Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

Home / FAQ
How do I download my purchased files? +

After completing your purchase, you'll receive an email with a download link. You can also access your files anytime from your account dashboard. Files are available for instant download 24/7.

Can I edit the name plates with my child's name? +

Yes! Most of our name plates come with editable Canva templates or PowerPoint files. You can easily customize the name, colors, and other elements to make it truly personal.

What file formats are included? +

Our products typically include: PDF files (A4 and US Letter sizes), editable Canva template links, high-resolution PNG files (300 DPI), and sometimes PowerPoint or Word documents for easy editing.

Can I print these at home? +

Absolutely! All our printables are designed for home printing. We recommend using good quality paper (cardstock works great for name plates) and a color printer for best results.

Do you offer refunds? +

Due to the digital nature of our products, we generally don't offer refunds once files are downloaded. However, if you experience any issues with your purchase, please contact us and we'll work to resolve it.

Can I use these for commercial purposes? +

Our standard license is for personal use only. If you need commercial use (like for a classroom, business, or resale), please contact us about our commercial licensing options.

Do you create custom designs? +

Yes! We offer custom design services for special occasions, events, or unique themes. Please contact us through our contact page with your requirements and we'll provide a quote.

How long do I have access to my downloads? +

Your downloads are available forever! Once purchased, you can access your files anytime from your account. We recommend saving a copy to your device or cloud storage for safekeeping.
